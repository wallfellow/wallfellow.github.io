About

Framed originals and quiet pieces for the wall. Hang it up.

The work

Wallfellow sells original wall pieces, framed and ready to hang. Frames are mostly thrifted and already finished, glass as they come. Each one is checked before it is listed.

Sizes stay small for now: about 8.5 × 11 inches framed, often smaller. Anything larger is for later.

Framed originals come first. A wall ornament or a vase may sit beside them.

Buying one

The catalog here is a placeholder. Pieces go up on Etsy as they are ready.

Original framed works are final sale. If your piece arrives damaged or differs from the listing due to our error, contact us within 7 days of delivery with clear photos of the packaging and artwork — we’ll repair, replace, or refund.

Orders ship in 3–5 business days.
